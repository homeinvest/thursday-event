Questions?

Who is this for?

Accredited investors who want tax-advantaged places to put their capital, without owning or managing property. We’ll reveal the exact multifamily real estate strategies we’re deploying today where write-offs are coming back larger than the amount raised, and show you how to apply the same strategy before December 31.

Do I have to be an accredited investor to attend?

Yes. To qualify, you generally need to earn more than $200,000 on your own (or $300,000 with your spouse) in each of the last two years, or have a net worth over $1 million not counting your primary home.

Can I bring my CPA?

Absolutely. The content we’ll share is designed to help your CPA. Bring your questions and theirs, and your CPA can confirm how it fits your situation.

I believe in paying my fair share. Is this for me?

Of course, and so do we. Everything we share is 100% legal, documented and used by the wealthiest investors, family offices, and institutions. We believe in paying a fair share, but not leaving a tip. Most investors work too hard to not use that money to fund their future, their family, and their freedom.

Do I need to be a real estate or tax expert to get value?

Not at all. We’ll break down everything in plain language that makes sense. If you’re a high-earner looking to owe the IRS less this year, we’ll give you actionable insights that you can start using today.

Why attend live?

Because a replay can’t answer your questions. This is a live so you can ask any question you have about how to reduce your taxes using the strategies we discuss. Whether you’re a first time or seasoned investor, this is for you.
